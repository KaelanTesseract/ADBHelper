# ADBHelper 0.4.8

This update fixes Daily interruptions during equipment upgrades, Dura’s Trials, reward collection, and shop purchases.

## Heroes and battles

- **Equip New Equipment** recognizes highlighted class icons and reads the equipment panel heading separately when full-screen text recognition misses it. It also recognizes the “No equipment to replace” message after Quick Equip.
- **Collect Affinity** uses two center-screen taps per hero. Nameplate and profile checks help it move through animated heroes, equipped skins, and max-level profiles. The activity counter shows heroes visited.
- **Dura’s Trials** follows the visible **Battle** or **Sweep** button even when the floor number is unreadable. It verifies the quantity and daily-token cost before confirming a batch of up to six Sweeps.
- **AFK Stages** opens the stage selector from the Apex Stage shortcut in the world view and closes the idle rewards popup when present.

## Daily rewards and purchases

- **Dream Realm** returns to the overview after checking rankings. Daily counts automatically collected ranking rewards when the gift badge disappears without a reward popup.
- **Guild Quest rewards** can continue after closing Noble Path returns directly to the world view. **Season Phase Ranking rewards** also handles phases without a First Clear section.
- **Diamond hourglasses** count earlier purchases toward the configured purchase count and diamond budget, so eligible remaining offers can still be bought. The next price tier or depleted allowance confirms each purchase.
- **Emporium purchases** verifies the discounted Invite Letter charge after returning to the Guild Store. It shows the offer price and diamond budget when the offer is over budget and skips sold-out offers or purchases already confirmed by the Daily Quest.

## Update

Open the green update icon beside **Settings**, review the patch notes, and select **Download and install**. You can also download **ADBHelper-Windows-x64.exe** from this release.
