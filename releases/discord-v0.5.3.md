## ADBHelper v0.5.3 is out! 🎣

**Fishing, start to finish**
Start **Fishing** anywhere in the game world and ADBHelper does the rest:
1️⃣ searches every unlocked map for fishing spots
2️⃣ walks to each spot
3️⃣ fishes, tapping the bite bar right in the middle for bigger fish
4️⃣ stops by itself when no spot has attempts left

Still a preview, so keep an eye on the emulator.

Update from the green icon beside **Settings**, or download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** from GitHub.

https://github.com/KaelanTesseract/ADBHelper/releases/tag/v0.5.3
