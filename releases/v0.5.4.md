# ADBHelper 0.5.4

**Fishing** now also starts from your Homestead.

## Fixed

- Starting **Fishing** while you were in your Homestead stopped with "Could not verify the world map", because Homestead has no minimap. ADBHelper now taps **World** first, waits until the world has loaded, and then opens the map as usual.

## Validation

- **209 automated tests passed**, together with formatting, Clippy, and license and advisory checks. An emulator run started in Homestead, returned to the world, and found all six fishing spots on Novaris again.

## Update

Click the green update icon beside **Settings**, review the patch notes, and choose **Download and install**. New here? Download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** below. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for a new download; choose **More info → Run anyway**.
