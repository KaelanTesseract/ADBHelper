## ADBHelper v0.4.4 is out! ✨

Build your own **AFK Journey daily routine** with the new **Daily** card.

⚙️ Open its gear, drag in the actions you want, and arrange their order. Your routine is saved automatically.

**What's new:**
• Collect AFK chest rewards **twice** for daily progress.
• Claim up to **3 available free hourglasses**.
• Add **diamond hourglasses** with your own purchase count and total budget per run.
• Add **AFK stage rewards** and existing combat actions to the same routine.
• Track collections with separate counters, without affecting combat statistics.

Diamond purchases are optional. Their initial limits are **1 purchase / 50 diamonds per run**; place free hourglasses first.

Already using ADBHelper? Click the **green update icon beside Settings** to install the update.
