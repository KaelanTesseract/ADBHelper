## ADBHelper v0.4.5 is out! ✨

**More rewards. Smoother runs.**

🎁 **New Daily option: Season Phase Ranking rewards**
Add it in the Daily gear popup to collect available **District, Server, and Guild** first-clear rewards. Each scope gets its own collection counter.

🔄 **Improved automatic recovery**
Reconnect after ADB interruptions, restart the game when needed, and resume the current mode or tower while keeping confirmed progress and attempt budgets.

⚔️ **Supreme Arena fixes**
Improved Continue → opponent selection → team preparation, support for already open formations, and correct closing of Daily Ranking Rewards.

⌛ **Dura's Trials fixes**
Arcane and Focused Treasure now support wrapped floor labels in the Sweep dialog. Up to **6 daily Sweeps** are still allowed only at a verified maximum stage, with quantity and cost checks.

✅ **130 automated tests passed**, plus live recovery, reward collection, Supreme Arena navigation, and six-Sweep checks. The Supreme Arena checks reached Battle without starting a fight.

**Update:** click the green update icon beside **Settings**, or download the Windows EXE here:
https://github.com/KaelanTesseract/ADBHelper/releases/tag/v0.4.5

Your saved routine and settings are retained. Add the new ranking-rewards step manually if you want to use it.
