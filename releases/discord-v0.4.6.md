## ADBHelper v0.4.6 is out! ✨

🧩 **Easier Daily routines**
Drop a step onto the area below the list to send it straight to the end. The list scrolls as you drag near either edge, and existing steps also have a **To end** button.

🎁 **Season Phase Ranking rewards**
ADBHelper checks the gift badges for **District**, **Server**, and **Guild**. When a tab has rewards but no individual Claim button is visible, it uses **Quick Claim**.

⚔️ **Supreme Arena**
Daily skips Supreme Arena during **Off Season** and moves on to the next step.

⌛ **Dura’s Trials**
Fixed stage recognition and Sweep dialog checks for **Cooperative Treasure** and **Ironwall Treasure**.

✅ **136 automated tests passed.** Live checks covered reward collection, Off Season skipping, and free first clears through stage 30.

Update from the green icon beside **Settings** or download the Windows EXE.
