# ADBHelper 0.5.6

**Union Campaign** now tries every team in the Records instead of stopping after three.

## Fixed

- When two players in the Records used the very same team, ADBHelper thought it could not page any further and stopped trying teams. On some floors this ended the run after only three teams. ADBHelper now also checks the player shown above the team, so it pages through the whole list. This applies wherever teams are copied from the Records.

## Validation

- **211 automated tests passed**, together with formatting, Clippy, and license and advisory checks. On the emulator, ADBHelper paged through all nine Records teams of a Union Campaign floor that previously stopped after three.

## Update

Click the green update icon beside **Settings**, review the patch notes, and choose **Download and install**. New here? Download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** below. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for a new download; choose **More info → Run anyway**.
