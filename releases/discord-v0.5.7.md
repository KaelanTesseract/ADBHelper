## ADBHelper v0.5.7 is out! ⚔️

**Hero Box**
A new tab shows all your heroes with portrait, ascension and EX weapon level. One click puts a picture of your box on the clipboard, ready to paste here. Heroes are recognized by the title above their name; anything unclear is listed for you to pick instead of being guessed.

**Also fixed**
Settings now stay after a restart, and Battle Pass rewards on Fabled Road are claimed again.

Update from the green icon beside **Settings**, or download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** from GitHub.

https://github.com/KaelanTesseract/ADBHelper/releases/tag/v0.5.7
