# ADBHelper 0.4.9

This test release introduces two AFK Journey scanners and fixes navigation and level recognition in Daily.

## New scanners

- **AFKJ Tracker Scan** exports hero ascension and exclusive weapon levels in the AFKJ Tracker backup format.
- **Guild Manager Scan** exports Dream Realm, Supreme Arena, and active Season AFK rankings, plus guild activeness. Choose what to scan in the action settings. The optional Supabase connection reads the guild roster to match member names and IDs.
- Both scanners use the game's English interface. Exports are saved under `%APPDATA%\ADBHelper\scans`. Use **Copy export path** on the action card to find the files.

## Scanner test status

- The hero scan produced a partial export in emulator testing. The latest guild activeness test exported 64 records for a guild with 72 listed members. Review files marked `partial` before importing them.
- Dream Realm ranking data was exported in the emulator. Supreme Arena and Season AFK ranking rows were checked with game captures. Live verification against a configured Supabase endpoint is pending.

## Bug fixes

- **Dream Realm** opens from Battle Modes both with and without the Guild Duel banner above the mode cards.
- **Level Up All Heroes** reads the Resonance Level at a higher magnification before and after each upgrade. It also recognizes the Resonating Hall when full-screen text recognition misses the roster heading. An upgrade is verified with two matching readings.
