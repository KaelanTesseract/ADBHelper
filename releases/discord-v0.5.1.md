## ADBHelper v0.5.1 is out! 🔌

**MuMu Player connects by itself**
ADBHelper now finds MuMu Player on its own, including **MuMu Player 12** and installs on other drives. It also tries all of MuMu's usual ports, so "No device connected" should be a thing of the past. No ADB path to enter.

Still no device? Start MuMu Player first, then press **Find devices** in **Settings**.

Update from the green icon beside **Settings**, or download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** from GitHub.

https://github.com/KaelanTesseract/ADBHelper/releases/tag/v0.5.1
