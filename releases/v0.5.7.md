# ADBHelper 0.5.7

The new **Hero Box** shows all your heroes with their ascension and EX weapon level and turns them into a picture for Discord. Settings now stay after a restart, and Battle Pass rewards on Fabled Road are collected again.

## Hero Box

- A new **Hero Box** tab next to **Settings** lists every hero with a portrait, the ascension (L+, M+, S, S+, P1–P4) and the EX weapon level. Filter it by faction and class.
- **Scan ascension and EX weapon** walks through your heroes in the Resonating Hall and reads both values. Set the hall's faction filter to **All**, because the scan only sees the heroes the hall shows.
- **Create picture** copies a picture of the shown heroes to the clipboard, ready to paste into Discord. It is also saved as `hero-box.png` in `%APPDATA%\ADBHelper`.
- ADBHelper recognizes each hero by the title above its name, such as "Misty Scorpion". It never guesses. If a hero cannot be named for certain, it appears under **Not recognized** with its picture. Pick who it is once, or add a hero that no list knows yet, and later scans recognize it.
- The installer brings the hero portraits along. If you update through the app, missing portraits are downloaded once.

## Hero database

- **Settings → Hero database → Scan hero database** also reads each hero's current stats and skills into `%APPDATA%\ADBHelper\heroes.json`, together with every hero of the game, including those you do not own yet. This prepares later features such as a battle simulation.

## Fixed

- **Settings are kept after a restart.** Before, only the Daily routine was saved, and action settings returned to their defaults.
- **Battle Pass rewards** stopped with "Quick Claim button could not be verified" on Fabled Road, because the button's italic lettering over the reward art could not be read. ADBHelper now also finds the button by its look and still claims only when it is green.

## Validation

- **278 automated tests passed**, together with formatting, Clippy, and license and advisory checks. Hero recognition was checked against a full stored hero list and recorded hero profiles. The Quick Claim fix was checked on the Fabled Road screen that had failed.

## Update

Click the green update icon beside **Settings**, review the patch notes, and choose **Download and install**. New here? Download **ADBHelper-Setup-x64.exe** below. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for a new download; choose **More info → Run anyway**.
